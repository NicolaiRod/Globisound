# Globisound – Live World Radio & Video Streams

**Globisound** is a lightweight, feature-rich desktop application for Windows that lets you explore and stream thousands of live radio stations and video streams from around the globe.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🌍 Global Station Browser:** Easily search and filter radio stations by country, name, or music genre.
- **⭐ Favorites:** Mark your favorite stations with a star and sort them just like the main station list.
- **🎙 Record as MP3:** Record live radio to MP3 files, up to 5 stations at the same time, with an optional stop timer.
- **⏰ Scheduled Recordings:** Plan recordings for a specific date and time. They keep running even if you change channel.
- **🎵 Auto Shazam Song Identification:** Automatically detects and displays the currently playing track and artist in real time.
- **📜 Live Lyrics Search:** Fetch song lyrics on the fly with a single click.
- **🎨 Audio Spectrum Visualizer:** Real-time audio spectrum visualization with interactive particle physics.
- **📺 Video Radio & Stream Support:** Embedded video panel supporting video streams, `.m3u8`, and live feeds.
- **🌐 Multi-Language Interface:** Available in Danish, English, German, Spanish, and French.
- **🎨 Customizable Themes:** Choose between multiple color themes (Dark Mode, Cyberpunk, Ocean Blue, etc.).

## What's New in v2.1.0

## ⭐ Favorites
- **Star your stations:** A new ★ column in the station list lets you mark or unmark a favorite with one click.
- **Favorites view:** Switch between **Stations**, **Favorites** and **Recordings** using the tabs above the list.
- **Sorting:** Click any column heading to sort, also in the favorites list.
- Your favorites are saved between sessions.

## 🎙 Recording
- **Record to MP3:** Press **⏺ Record** to record the selected station. Press it again to stop.
- **Up to 5 simultaneous recordings** from different stations, independent of what you are listening to.
- **Stop timer:** Choose to stop automatically after 15, 30, 60, 120 or 180 minutes.
- **Scheduled recordings:** Use **⏰ Schedule…** to set a start date and time and a duration. Scheduled recordings keep running when you change channel, as long as Globisound is open.
- **Choose where recordings are saved:** Pick any folder with **📁 Choose folder…** (default: `Music\Globisound`).
- **Saved recordings:** Play, save a copy, open the folder or delete recordings from the **Recordings** tab. Deleting a recording permanently removes the file from your disk.
- MP3 streams are saved directly without re-encoding. Other stream formats are converted to MP3.

## ⚠️ Recording Notice
Recording is at your own risk. It is your own responsibility to find out whether it is legal to record from a given station and in your country, and what you may use the recording for. Recordings can normally only be used privately and must not be shared or published. Globisound and its developer accept no responsibility for how the feature is used. The app asks you to confirm this the first time you record.

## 🌍 Features & Improvements
- **Real-Time Day/Night Terminator on 3D Globe:** Added a dynamic interactive 3D globe including day/night light shader. The shadow line automatically updates based on current UTC solar calculations to show real-time daylight and dusk worldwide.
